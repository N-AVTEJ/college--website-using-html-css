History of Aurora University

Aurora University was established with a vision to provide quality education and foster innovation in various fields of study.

  • Foundation Year: Aurora College was founded to cater to the growing demand for technical and professional education.
  • Expansion: Over the years, new departments and courses were introduced, covering fields like Engineering, Sciences, Management, and Fine Arts.
  • Research & Innovation: The college became a hub for cutting-edge research, attracting students and faculty from across the country.
  • Global Recognition: With strategic collaborations and industry tie-ups, Aurora College has gained recognition as a center of higher learning and skill development.
